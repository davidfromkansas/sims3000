# Explain the demand bars

City desk → Zone development now includes a demand breakdown for all three sectors. It shows baseline demand, jobs or resident needs, existing population/workplaces, abandoned buildings, tax rates, operating rewards, city approval, congestion, regional access and population/job capacity limits. Range adjustments explain where values are capped.

The ledger is generated by the simulation’s own calculations rather than a separate approximation. Its entries reconcile to each citywide demand bar. Commercial and industrial tiles can still differ because regional access varies locally; the existing development list supplies local demand.

Feedback checkpoint: can you identify whether your next growth problem calls for jobs, a budget change, transport improvements or more growth capacity?

Validation: 275 automated suites pass, including ledger reconciliation across empty, starter and capacity-limited cities, taxes, saved continuation and read-only inspection.
