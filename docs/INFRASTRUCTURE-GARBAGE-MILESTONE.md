# Power and recycling disposal planning

Power plants now need a garbage collection route. Coal, oil, gas, nuclear, microwave and fusion plants each generate 8 units per month per intact building; wind and solar add none. Collection uses roads beside the entire footprint. Damaged plants stop generating new garbage, while existing waste remains collectable. This is a waste rule; it does not change electrical generation during damage.

Operating recycling centers contribute .72 units per month to the existing recycling/disposal system. They can recover the eligible share of their own new waste, but the remainder still needs landfill, incineration or export. Their production follows existing power and road readiness.

Utilities and inspection show each facility's monthly production and backlog. All infrastructure production enters the same city totals, saved garbage queues, pollution and monthly conservation accounting as other sources.

Rates follow table 14-2, pages 216–217, of the SimCity 3000 Unlimited Prima guide: plant weight 800 and recycling weight 72, divided by 100. Per-month scale, fixed per-building output and readiness rules are reconstruction calibration. No save-schema migration is required.
