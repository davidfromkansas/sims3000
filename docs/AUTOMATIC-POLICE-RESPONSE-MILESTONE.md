# Automatic police response

Working stations now automatically send available squads to a riot within their funded coverage radius. Manually dispatched station squads stay at their positions and cannot simultaneously respond at home. Volunteers remain manually controlled. The map labels automatic response and Emergency reports show the squad count and availability rules.

Readiness checks live footprint damage, power, road access/condition, funding and strike state. Both manual and automatic station responses use funding and jail effectiveness. Response is immediate; strength/timing are reconstruction tuning. Existing dispatch slots remain volunteer first, then stations in map order; persistent station identities across station removal are still a future refinement. No additional dispatch orders are written for automatic responses, and no save schema change is required (134).

The official strategy guide, printed pp.328–329, describes automatic response inside a precinct, manual dispatch beyond it and the inability of an away squad to also serve its home precinct: https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide .
