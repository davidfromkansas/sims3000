# University reward progression

A city now earns the university at its first monthly evaluation with education at least 70 on the existing 0–100 scale. Population, approval, cash flow and the presence of a college are not additional gates. The offer remains available after education declines, and construction is free. Unique placement, upkeep, power/road operation and 500 commuter jobs remain supported.

The official Prima guide gives university eligibility as EQ 105, no population or aura prerequisite, and zero construction price (printed p.417). Its introductory EQ overview identifies maximum EQ as 150. This reconstruction normalizes that threshold to 70/100. The six-month wait was invented and is removed. The simulation still evaluates offers monthly.

Source: https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide

Schema 131 preserves already earned offers and existing campuses. Legacy university streaks up to six are validated before conversion; unearned old streaks reset because their rules no longer apply. Current saves accept at most one qualifying university month. The campus remains the existing 4×4 footprint; the original 10×10 campus, original education model and full proximity effects require further work.
