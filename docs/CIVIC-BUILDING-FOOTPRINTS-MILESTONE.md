# Civic building footprints

New civic construction reserves 3 × 3 lots for police, fire, hospitals, schools, jails, colleges and museums, and 2 × 2 lots for libraries. Placement previews and the Civic services catalog show the required size. Each lot renders once in all four camera views.

Inspect any member to review the whole facility. Budgets, jobs, education capacity, emergency crews and growth-cap relief count a facility once. Road access is collected across the lot; all members must have power and be free of damage to operate. Station coverage is centered on the lot. Demolition from any member removes the facility, and active fire prevents demolition anywhere in its footprint.

Save version 129 stores the civic lot size and validates complete, level, consistently rooted footprints. Earlier cities retain one-tile civic buildings, with a one-tile size stored on migration; surrounding roads and homes are preserved. Rebuilding uses the full-size lot. Total electricity consumption is spread over the footprint, preserving per-facility demand.

The learning challenge now identifies suitable library and museum sites for their full sizes and still supports a normal-construction win, including saved continuation.

Evidence: [official Prima guide](https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide), civic building directories, printed pp.323, 332, 340 and 351–353. Service prices, capacities, coverage formulas and legacy compact artwork remain reconstruction approximations where not separately updated.
