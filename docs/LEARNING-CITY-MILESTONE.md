# A city that learns

A new prepared challenge makes the adult education system playable through a two-year objective. Start with 224 residents and §8,000, then reach adult EQ 41, library and museum access of 90% each, at least 224 residents, and a §1,000 reserve. The briefing points to suitable building sites and explains funding, time, aging and migration.

Roads, power and landfill collection are prepared. The challenge uses normal construction and monthly simulation, with no scripted EQ awards. It is an original scenario, not a shipped SimCity 3000 scenario.

Validation: ordinary library and museum construction wins before the deadline; a saved mid-challenge city continues identically; neglect and building only libraries lose. Setup is deterministic and initial finances, population and waste are checked.

Feedback: launch A city that learns in Scenarios, build the facilities, and watch the separate access and adult EQ goals progress.
