# Civic service disaster shutdowns

A burning hospital previously continued to provide 100% healthcare coverage. All eight civic facility types now stop providing coverage and capacity while burning, destroyed, or contaminated by radiation. Their inspection panels explain the damage condition. Service resumes once damage is cleared and power, road access, funding and strike conditions permit.

This applies to police, fire, hospitals, schools, jails, colleges, libraries and museums. Existing department maintenance billing continues during an outage. The change affects automatic service coverage; it does not revise the separate manual emergency-unit allocation system.

Validation covers all eight facilities against all three damage conditions, active counts, service capacities, response radii, inspection reasons and recovery. An actual ignited hospital loses healthcare coverage and remains shut down after save/load.

Feedback: ignite a hospital or school in a disposable city, inspect the service loss, extinguish the fire, and check recovery.

This milestone remains local/GitHub only while automatic approval review blocks Sites publication.
