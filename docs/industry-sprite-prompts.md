# Industry sprites
Built-in Imagegen, 2172×724 PNG with alpha, five columns/one row. Inspected complete silhouettes without background halos. First generation/cleanup outputs with halos or baked checkerboard were rejected. Selected image copied unchanged into dist/assets/industry-atlas.png; runtime cell alpha trimming. Slots 52–56: barn, crops, low/medium/high clean industry.

Prompt:
Create a transparent PNG atlas of FIVE isolated isometric city-builder sprites in a SINGLE HORIZONTAL ROW, FIVE equal columns, wide landscape composition. Left to right: red-roof farm barn with silo on a field diamond; crop field diamond with rows of crops only; small clean-tech research workshop; medium clean-tech factory with glass office wing; large gleaming high-tech industrial complex. Exactly five sprites, strict equal columns. Complete separate silhouettes with wide transparent margins and no clipping. Classic muted late-1990s realistic 3D-looking 2D game sprites. Orthographic 2:1 isometric ground diamonds, top-left lighting. Genuinely transparent alpha background with hard cutout silhouettes. No text, labels, grid, logos, people, scenery, glow, mist or shadows beyond each diamond footprint. All five sprites small enough to be fully contained in their own cells.
