# First-town service guidance

Players can now follow eight checks from construction to a functioning town. The guide adds delivered water, 90% employment access, a completed garbage collection month without a backlog, and three consecutive surplus months to roads, zoning, electricity, and population growth. Buttons open construction tools or the relevant utilities, commuting and budget reports. Scenario cities retain their scenario goals and status panel.

Checks use current simulation data and can regress. Electricity counts delivered supply, including connected imports; idle capacity cannot satisfy an outage. Garbage requires production and actual disposal in the current month's history. The introductory employment and budget targets are explicitly identified as this recreation's exercise, not recovered original victory rules. No save schema change.

Validation: all 215 suites passed. An empty-map fixture built through normal engine construction satisfies all eight checks after nine months. Regression tests cover missing service, unemployment, stale waste history, zero production, deficit streaks, report callbacks and scenario routing. Local browser QA verified the eight-goal dialog and commuting, utility and budget shortcuts, with no warnings or errors. Existing local city remained paused and unmodified.

Feedback checkpoint: start on empty land, follow the two phases, and check whether the service reports explain what to build next clearly enough.
