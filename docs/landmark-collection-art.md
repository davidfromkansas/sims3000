# Landmark collection art provenance

Imagegen produced original Statue of Liberty and Big Ben gallery illustrations with the prompts: premium isometric city-builder miniature, full recognizable monument on a compact square plaza, orthographic 2:1 ground projection, daylight upper left, isolated object. The transparency requests returned background haze; subsequent cutout requests produced baked checkerboards and were discarded. Only the two original illustrations are included, as gallery art.

City drawings are independently authored polygon geometry in `landmark-models.js`, with stone tiers, windows, clocks, copper robe, raised torch and crown. They do not edit or raster-cut the generated images. The meshes project into four cached Canvas views. Neither illustrations nor models reproduce original game assets.
