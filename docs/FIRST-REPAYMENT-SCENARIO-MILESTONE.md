# The first repayment

## Player-visible step change

Scenarios & status now offers an eighth prepared challenge: manage a town through its first annual loan payment. The briefing and starting map establish 224 residents, §2,500 cash, a §25,000 loan and prepared garbage collection. In twelve months, pay §3,750 and finish with at least 224 residents, 80% road condition and §500 cash. Additional borrowing disqualifies the attempt. The original loan still has nine annual payments after victory.

Budget and Mortimer’s treasury outlook provide the tools to plan a response. Existing restart, save/load, status and sandbox continuation controls work with the new challenge.
