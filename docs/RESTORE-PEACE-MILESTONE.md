# Restore the peace

A new original prepared challenge starts with a riot, two police squads dispatched away and Police funding reduced to 25%. The town has 224 residents, §2,500, police/fire stations, a jail and landfill collection. Players can recall or reposition squads, restore funding, contain resulting fires and rebuild. Victory requires three consecutive monthly checks with no active emergency or rubble, 224 residents, crime at most 15 and §1,500 within twelve months.

Emergency-start scenarios now begin held, with a matching notice and the police dispatch tool selected for riots. Players can inspect before continuing. Normal scenarios retain their paused Play flow.

Verified playthrough: restore Police funding to 100% and recall both squads; contain the emergency in eight response steps, then win in month three with 280 residents and §2,452. Saving mid-emergency and continuing reproduces the same result. Leaving funding at 25% loses; renewed underfunding resets a recovery streak. Preview and fresh restart preserve initial conditions. The actual start callback is exercised for emergency and ordinary scenarios.
