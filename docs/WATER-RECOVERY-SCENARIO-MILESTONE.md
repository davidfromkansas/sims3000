# Water under pressure milestone

A seventh prepared challenge, Water under pressure, is available from Scenarios. The town starts in 2000 with 224 residents, §10,000, a freshwater pump, an aging emergency tower, connected landfill collection and a broken trunk pipe at displayed coordinates 25, 17. The city has 530 units of water capacity and 179 demand, yet 88 units go unserved because the lower network cannot use the pump’s reserve.

Players can repair the missing pipe or arrange another viable supply. They must serve all water demand, preserve at least 224 residents and hold §5,000 for six consecutive monthly checks within two years. A failed monthly condition resets the streak. The goal includes a View goal area action to locate the break. Previously supplied buildings retain their water requirement, so prolonged neglect causes abandonment. Random disasters start disabled.

This is an original teaching scenario, not a shipped SimCity 3000 scenario file. It combines the manual’s prepared-city/scenario goals (pp.24–26, 35) with water connections, finite supply and continuity (pp.117–118). The layout, initial quantities, deadline and targets are authored for this reconstruction. All solutions use normal game construction and simulation.

Feedback exercise: open the briefing, start the challenge, inspect Utilities, and compare the wet and dry pipe networks. Restore water service and follow the six-month objective. Assess whether the global reserve versus local outage is understandable and whether the recovery deadline feels fair.

Validation: deterministic prepared city; active source and local shortage; exact starting population/reserve; ordinary pipe repair wins in six months; doing nothing loses at month 24; interruption resets progress and repair wins at month 10; a mid-recovery save reproduces the exact victory; duplicate status checks cannot advance progress.
