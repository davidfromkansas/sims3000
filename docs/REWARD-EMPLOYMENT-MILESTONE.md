# Stadium and university employment

Operating stadiums now provide 200 jobs and universities 500. This completes employment for the four currently implemented civic rewards. Positions use the shared finite worker pool and transport routes, contribute to residential demand, and disappear when the reward loses service. Reward cards and building queries show the new capacities and queries show positions filled.

Source: Prima official guide, printed p.158, non-RCI job creation table: https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide

Validation uses actual powered, road-connected four-tile-wide buildings and enough residents to fill all jobs. It checks finite capacity, footprint deduplication, traffic, service loss, recovery and saved employment.

Existing reward unlock rules, prices and demand bonuses remain reconstruction tuning. Utility and recreation jobs and the full reward catalog are still incomplete.

Feedback: place an earned university or stadium near a connected workforce, then inspect its filled positions and transport demand.
