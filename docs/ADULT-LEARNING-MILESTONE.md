# Complementary adult learning

Libraries and museums now provide independent learning benefits. Once a library fully covers a neighborhood, adding a museum still helps adults. Additional copies of either service cannot multiply that service’s full-coverage EQ rate.

The adult simulation applies compounded 1.91% annual decay, library gains of 0.09 EQ per month, museum gains of 0.07, and Pro Reading gains of 0.01. Facility gains scale with independently capped, resident-weighted road-connected coverage. The education report exposes both coverage values and the monthly gain.

Source: Prima official strategy guide, printed p.350, https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide . The reconstruction retains its 0–100 EQ range, eight age bands, existing service capacities and road-network coverage model; this is not full numeric parity with every original education system.

Feedback: build a library and museum, inspect the education report, and compare adult learning with either service missing or education funding suspended. Population movement still changes cohort averages.

Validation: actual facilities, separate coverage, duplicate saturation, reading, exact annual decay, saved continuation, and funding shutdown. Existing cohort fixtures updated to supply independent service coverage.
