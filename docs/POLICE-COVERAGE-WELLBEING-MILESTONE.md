# Police coverage and wellbeing

Police stations now cover a 30-tile radius at full funding. Protection decreases with distance but retains one quarter of its normalized maximum at the boundary. Insufficient jail capacity reduces effectiveness to 75% rather than 60%. Range gains taper above 110% funding using the existing reconstruction curve.

Overlapping precincts preserve their combined presence before coverage percentages are capped. Residential aura reports show a police-presence contribution, and station inspection flags occupied neighborhoods above optimal coverage. Removing excess stations or reducing funding can restore the wellbeing contribution.

The [official Prima guide, pp.259 and 325–327](https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide) describes the radius, distance falloff, jail dependence, optimal +20 aura and excessive-policing penalty up to −10. The precise curve remains reconstruction tuning: +20 at 100% presence, decreasing continuously to −10 at 200%, capped there. Arrest/crime ratios, original jail-overcrowding mechanics and strike formulas remain future fidelity work.
