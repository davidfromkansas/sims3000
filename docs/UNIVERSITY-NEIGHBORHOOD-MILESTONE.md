# A university with neighborhood tradeoffs

An operating university now has local air/water pollution and crime pressure alongside its employment, education, demand and neighborhood benefits. The reward panel and campus inspection explain these tradeoffs. Residential land value receives a smaller direct benefit than commercial value; industry receives none. Nearby homes also receive a small direct aura contribution. Police mitigate campus-related crime with the rest of local crime.

The previous uniform amenity bonus is replaced by sector-specific effects. Every footprint tile must remain intact and powered, with working road access, for campus effects to apply. Damage or service loss removes the operating effects. Existing 4 × 4 legacy campuses retain their stored footprint; new campuses remain 10 × 10. Save schema remains 136.

## Source and approximations

[Prima guide, printed page 417](https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide) lists university land-value effects 5/15/0, air pollution 6,000 within 10 tiles, water pollution 6,000 within 5 tiles, crime 15 within 10 tiles, aura 1 within 20 tiles and 500 jobs. Pollution intensities are divided by 100 for this model; aura uses the normalized −127…127 scale. Linear spatial falloff, a 10-tile land-value radius, and how police mitigate campus pressure are explicit reconstruction approximations. Existing education/demand bonuses remain tuned behavior.

## Ordinary-play progression

The ordinary metropolis continues from the prior 151,720-resident checkpoint. Connected hospitals, schools, colleges, libraries and museums are funded normally, and residential taxes are set to zero through the regular budget controls. Commercial/industrial tax income covers ongoing costs. No grants, loans or direct population/education edits are used.

The city earns Stadium and County Courthouse at month 368, Medical Research Center after its introduction, and University at month 545 with 171,720 residents, 70.0327 EQ and §16,427,469. The earned campus was placed on 100 tiles for no building charge; §245 bought its road/power connections and all 500 jobs filled. This proves one path to the University in the current simulation. It does not prove original balance, maximum education, indefinite stability or all map/difficulty settings.
