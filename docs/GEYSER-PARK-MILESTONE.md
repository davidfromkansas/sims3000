# Geyser Park reward

Players can earn and defer a Geyser Park offer by maintaining 35 park tiles and strong approval at a monthly evaluation. The checklist counts small-park tiles and the nine tiles of each large park; this area-based interpretation is explicit reconstruction calibration. Other recreation types do not count. The saved offer survives later eligibility loss and permits rebuilding after demolition.

The unique 5×5 reward costs §10,000 with no monthly upkeep. An operating park supplies 50 accessible jobs, adds 25,000 residential capacity, improves nearby land value and approval, and reduces air and water pollution. It produces 10 garbage units/month and requires the existing reward electricity/road access rules. All benefits and new waste stop when operation fails.

The [Prima reward directory](https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide), page 411, supplies the prerequisites, footprint, cost, jobs, capacity and neighborhood values. Source aura 100 maps to 89.37 on this game's approval scale. Land-value additions are 5/15/5 for residential/commercial/industrial zones; the 15-tile value radius is an approximation. Pollution reductions use source −2500/15 normalized to −25/15; aura uses 1/20 normalized by 100/254. Table 14-2 supplies garbage weight 1000, normalized to 10/month.

The original four-view model depicts mineral terraces, a turquoise pool, timber walks and a visitor lodge. A depth-tested ballistic water plume indicates operation on the existing scenery clock, without new timers or DOM animation. Pause freezes that clock; reduced motion and disabled scenery animation use a fixed pose; inactive parks and data layers omit the plume. Contact-sheet review verified four views and two sample poses. Browser performance and motion feel remain unverified while the Mac is locked.

Save schema 142 introduces the reward. Earlier cities receive an unearned offer slot; older-version files cannot claim the new reward or footprint.

Validation covers both eligibility boundaries, mixed small/large park area, durable offers, placement cost/uniqueness, jobs/capacity, sector land value, pollution and aura, damage/rebuilding, save migration, model bounds, renderer alignment and plume gating. The ordinary large city earned the offer at month 628 and built it at (90,91), providing land-value benefit to 24 residential tiles.
