# Sawtooth factory artwork

Small dirty-industry buildings gain an original sawtooth-roof factory variant. Mixed factory buildings in City view is enabled by default, distributes it deterministically across single-tile low-density factories, and can be disabled. Explicit replacements and custom designs take priority. Clean industry, farms, offices, larger lots and higher development levels retain their existing automatic appearances.

Players can also choose Sawtooth factory through building-style replacement and carry that choice in city saves/building sets. Larger-lot replacements retain procedural geometry with the dirty-industry palette. No population, jobs, demand, pollution or save schema changes (135).

A single built-in Imagegen request creates the transparent asset. The native PNG is preserved without programmatic editing. Tests cover deterministic selection, history/abandonment, exclusions, replacement persistence, building sets, customization priority and designer defaults.
