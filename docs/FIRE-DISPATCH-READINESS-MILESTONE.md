# Fire dispatch readiness

Dispatch capacity now excludes burning, destroyed and radiation-contaminated fire stations. The volunteer unit remains available. Capacity is shared by dispatch limits, emergency statistics and response-step crew recall, so a damaged station cannot supply an extra crew. Recovery restores capacity.

The manual tutorial describes one volunteer plus one additional unit per fire station. This preserves that model and the existing professional suppression scaling with fire funding and strikes. Dispatch does not require the automatic service-coverage rules; intact stations still count independently of local power and road coverage. The emergency panel explains the distinction and cycling behavior.

Validation covers damage and recovery, volunteer relocation, saved readiness, automatic crew recall, and the existing fire playthrough suite. Radiation test fixtures use the actual boolean format.

Feedback: dispatch with a fire station, then ignite the station and check available units. The volunteer can still be moved around the fire.
