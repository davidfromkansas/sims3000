# Growing upward challenge

The eleventh prepared challenge teaches densification rather than adding more low-density land. Start with 224 residents, 10,000 in funds, roads, electricity and landfill. Within 24 months, reach 700 residents, develop at least 25 residential tiles to level 2 or higher, supply those homes with water and power, and retain 5,000.

The briefing gives two freshwater pumping sites and a pipe/power route, then asks players to upgrade all three RCI sectors to medium density. Residential growth alone runs out of jobs. This is original reconstruction content using the manual’s water, zoning density, demand and balanced development mechanics.

A playthrough using only construction tools and monthly simulation wins at month eight with 728 residents, 28 taller home tiles and 8,230 cash. Saving at month four and continuing produces identical state. Upgrading all zoning without water fails; adding water but upgrading only homes also fails. The scenario uses the existing preview, export, start, save and result flows.
