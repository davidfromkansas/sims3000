# Education across generations

Manual pages 112–113 distinguish teaching young residents from maintaining adult knowledge. The old aggregate target allowed libraries and museums to raise education directly. The new model stores separate young and adult EQ values. Schools and colleges change young EQ; libraries and museums reduce adult knowledge decay. Graduating residents gradually carry their schooling into the adult average. Electricity, network access, finite capacity, funding and strikes apply to both functions.

This is a two-group approximation with fixed 25% young/75% adult shares, not detailed age demographics. Rates and capacity coefficients are recreation tuning. Schema 30 migrates older cities by initializing both groups to their previous aggregate EQ, preserving the initial city average. New trends record both groups from the next simulated month.
