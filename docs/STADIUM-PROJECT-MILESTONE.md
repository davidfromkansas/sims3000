# Stadium as a major city project

The stadium now needs 150,000 residents, strong approval and a 75,000 construction budget. New stadiums occupy 5×5 tiles. The invented positive monthly balance gate and six-month wait are removed; the offer arrives at the next qualifying monthly evaluation. Earned offers survive later declines.

The official Prima stadium directory (printed p.416) gives population 150,000, aura 100, price 75,000 and size 5×5. The manual’s Scenario Creator appendix gives aura’s range as −127 to 127. This reconstruction’s 0–100 approval scale normalizes the aura threshold to (100+127)*100/254, approximately 89.37. This normalization is an adaptation to the current aura model, not a claim that the original formula has been recreated.

Schema 133 preserves old 4×4 stadiums and existing offers. Legacy six-month streaks are validated before conversion; unearned progress resets. New saves validate complete, uniform 4×4 or 5×5 lots and at most one qualifying stadium month. Rebuilding requires the new footprint and price.

Each operating stadium still provides 200 commuter jobs and 125,000 residential demand-cap relief. The current 25 monthly upkeep and other proximity/amenity effects remain reconstruction tuning. Artwork scales to the actual lot size.
