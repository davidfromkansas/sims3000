# Education capacity and budgeting

Education buildings now use the directory’s base capacities and costs:

| Building | Capacity at 100% funding | Construction | Monthly upkeep |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| School | 3,000 students | 500 | 30 |
| College | 7,500 students | 3,000 | 125 |
| Library | 41,000 | 1,000 | 50 |
| Museum | 83,000 | 1,500 | 75 |

The Civic services build table shows capacity and monthly upkeep, alongside the lot size and operating count. Facility inspection continues to compare real connected demand with available capacity. Additional facilities only help where demand exceeds the available service; disconnected neighborhoods cannot borrow capacity.

Evidence: [official Prima guide, printed pp.351–353](https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide), education building directory. The guide’s introductory school value differs; this implementation follows the directory’s 3,000-student value, also repeated in its education recommendations.

Current age groups, road catchments and linear capacity adjustment with department funding remain reconstruction approximations. Full original education grades and teacher-to-student effects are not claimed here. Existing cities immediately use the new derived capacity and operating costs; their buildings retain their locations and saved sizes.
