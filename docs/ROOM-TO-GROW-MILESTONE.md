# Room to grow

The ninth prepared challenge gives players a city already at the residential population cap. Its 96×96 starting map has 25,000 residents, 100 vacant home sites, 1,500 occupied industrial tiles, fully supplied electricity, connected roads and landfill, and §20,000 cash. Wind turbines stand on raised one-tile sites. Low-density development starts without water under the current simulation rules.

Within twelve months, players must raise residential capacity to 26,000, welcome at least 200 additional residents and retain §5,000. A clear 6×6 site begins at tile 86,3 near the northeast corner. Players can choose a large park there or another combination of capacity-raising amenities. The normal scenario preview, status, restart, export and terminal result mechanisms apply. Random disasters start off.

This is an original tutorial for the reconstruction's population-cap mechanics, not a recovered shipped scenario. It is deliberately a regular prepared grid and is not a claim of realistic urban form or original balance. The long-term city still needs broader civic and utility planning after the tutorial.

Validation: deterministic starting state, all occupied sites powered, connected landfill, a large park placed through the normal build API, victory in month one at 25,240 residents, zero uncollected waste, exact save/reload continuation, twelve-month loss without capacity relief and fresh restart. Browser review verified the starting preview and launched the city at 25,000 residents and §20,000. All 253 regression suites passed.
