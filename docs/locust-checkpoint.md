# Locust plague and crop dusters

Manual pages 58 and 110 describe up to three crop dusters spraying vegetation ahead of swarms or targeting the insects directly. Emergency now offers a manual locust plague with a moving vegetation-seeking swarm. Trees are stripped and farm development is lost; roads, utilities and other buildings remain. Farm zoning survives for redevelopment.

Dispatch up to three aircraft. Further orders retask the oldest assignment. Spray arrives after two response steps, affects a four-tile radius and lasts ten steps. Swarms weaken in treated areas; otherwise they continue for up to 36 response steps. Timing, radius, damage and movement are reconstruction choices, not original coefficients. Random locust outbreaks are optional and off by default. Additional disaster types remain unfinished.

The normal calendar waits for the emergency, panels pause the response clock, and saves preserve swarm and aircraft progress. Existing saves migrate without a plague. Feedback: spray ahead of an approaching swarm, then compare vegetation damage with an undefended copy of the same city.

Original swarm and aircraft sprites were generated with Imagegen. The atlas retains a faint colored halo. Browser verification confirmed sprite loading, spray coverage and containment; all 39 simulation suites pass.

Random outbreaks use the saved city seed and month. Vegetation is required; farms increase the provisional monthly chance from 0.3% to a maximum 1.5%. The UI displays the current chance and blocks changes during an active disaster. Schema 32 preserves older cities with random locusts off. Aircraft status distinguishes transit, active spraying and readiness; ready aircraft are reused before busy assignments.
