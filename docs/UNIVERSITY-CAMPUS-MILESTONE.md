# Full university campus

New universities occupy the original 10×10 footprint from the official Prima university directory (printed p.417). Players must reserve 100 clear, level land tiles. Hover placement, saved geometry, all four rendered views and inspection use the campus bounds. Road and rail commuter searches and nearby amenity effects use the actual saved campus size.

A campus remains one reward: one upkeep bill and 500 jobs, despite its 100 tiles. Damage or loss of power on any tile suspends its benefits. Demolition from any campus tile removes the entire campus; its earned offer supports rebuilding.

Schema 132 stores universitySize. Earlier cities retain their 4×4 campuses without modifying neighbors. New saves validate complete, uniform 4×4 or 10×10 footprints. Destroyed or demolished campuses clear the stored size. Rebuilding uses the new 10×10 footprint.
