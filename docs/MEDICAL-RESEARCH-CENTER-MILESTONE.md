# Medical Research Center

The sixth reward is a paid, unique 3 × 3 research campus with an original model that rotates with the city. From 1975 onward, a city with at least 80,000 residents, life expectancy of 70 and approval of 89.37007874/100 receives an offer at the next monthly evaluation. Placement costs §75,000. Earned offers persist through declining conditions and demolition; reconstruction costs the same amount.

An operating center provides 135 jobs requiring actual worker access, benefits nearby residential/commercial land value and wellbeing, and produces local air and water pollution. It does not provide hospital beds. Every footprint tile must be intact and powered, with road access and working roads. Reports explain both the unlock conditions and operating effects.

## Source and implementation limits

[Prima official guide, printed page 413](https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide) gives the year, population, footprint, price, life-expectancy prerequisite, 135 jobs, land-value effects 10/10/0, air 540/radius 10, water 720/radius 5 and aura 2/radius 15. The aura threshold is normalized from the manual’s −127…127 scale. Pollution intensity is divided by 100, consistent with the existing City Hall treatment. Linear spatial falloff and a 15-tile land-value radius are reconstruction tuning. The center has no monthly upkeep in this model; the guide’s maintenance table does not list a charge. No additional health multiplier is claimed.
