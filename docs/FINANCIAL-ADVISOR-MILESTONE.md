# Financial advisor and treasury outlook

## Player capability
Meet Mortimer Green from Budget & taxes. His original generated portrait accompanies a briefing on negative cash, operating deficits, loan reserves and policy costs. Open the budget or ordinances directly from his recommendations.

Choose a one-, two-, five- or ten-year horizon. Inspect each month's opening cash, operating balance, combined loan repayments and closing treasury. The briefing flags the first projected negative balance and lowest treasury, including cases where operating income is positive but an annual loan payment causes a shortage.

## Manual relationship and limits
The manual (pp.86–87) assigns financial advice and warnings about dwindling funds to Mortimer. Its loan terms are ten annual installments totaling 150% of principal (p.91). This outlook uses the reconstruction's current operating budget and actual installment schedule, rather than claiming to recover the original advisor dialogue or forecasting algorithm.

Income and expenses remain fixed at current city conditions. Construction, future growth, changing demographics and land value, contract expiry and penalties, relief grants and disasters are excluded. The longer horizon illustrates commitments, not a prediction of future city development. Month-end balances do not represent every transaction within a month. Viewing the advisor advances no time and applies no financial changes.

## Feedback checkpoint
1. Open Budget & taxes → Meet Mortimer and inspect the next year's balances.
2. Compare a loan repayment month against adjacent months; extend the horizon to see maturities.
3. Follow his budget or ordinance shortcut, adjust a policy or budget, then revisit the outlook.

Tests compare all 120 projected months with actual settlement of staggered loans through maturity, verify year-end forecast agreement, and cover initial debt, future shortages with positive operating income, recurring deficits, policy advice, portrait PNG dimensions, read-only state preservation and UI horizon/navigation callbacks.
