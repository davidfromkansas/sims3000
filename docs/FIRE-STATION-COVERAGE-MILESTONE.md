# Fire station coverage

At full funding, an operating fire station now protects a 25-tile radius uniformly, including the boundary. Overlapping stations contribute additional suppression during actual fires. Coverage percentages remain bounded at 100%; a separate derived protection value preserves overlapping station strength.

The city map and station inspection explain uniform protection and overlapping responses. Lower funding shrinks the coverage area. Range increases taper above 110% funding, using a reconstruction curve of square-root scaling with one tenth of additional funding above that threshold. Existing strike and funding-effectiveness rules remain approximations.

Evidence: [official Prima guide, printed pp.321–322](https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide), which describes the 25-tile radius, uniform fire protection, additive overlap and limited extra range above 110% funding. This milestone does not claim full parity for aura from excess coverage, original strike behavior or dispatch-unit mechanics.

Feedback checkpoint: view the fire map, compare coverage near the station and at the edge, then overlap a second station over a vulnerable area. Reduce funding and watch the uncovered area expand.

Validation covers exact coverage boundaries, uniform strength, additional suppression of a real fire, funding, strikes and saved reconstruction.
