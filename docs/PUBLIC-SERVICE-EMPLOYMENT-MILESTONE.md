# Public-service employment

Operating police stations, fire stations, hospitals, schools, jails, colleges and museums each provide 45 jobs; libraries provide 20. These facilities join civic rewards in the finite commuting job pool. Their jobs affect residential demand, can be filled through road, bus or rail routes, and appear in civic employment totals and facility inspection. Workers cannot fill multiple jobs.

Source: Prima official guide, printed p.158, non-RCI job creation table: https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide

A shared operating check makes jobs and service coverage agree about funding, strikes, damage, road condition and power. It reads current conditions before transport runs, avoiding stale prior-month service flags. Positions are available while operating; they do not scale with funding above zero. Existing service footprints and capacity tuning remain. Utility and recreation employment are still incomplete.

Validation covers every service, actual road and rail commutes, finite vacancies, sector separation, damage and funding shutdowns, inspection and save/load. A broken rail line leaves the jobs available but unfilled.

Feedback: build public services near unemployed neighborhoods and inspect the resulting commutes, filled positions and remaining vacancies.
