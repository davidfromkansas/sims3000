# Justice district and reward inspection

An operating County Courthouse now improves nearby residential and commercial land value as well as reducing crime and providing jobs. Querying any residential, commercial or industrial zone exposes a collapsible breakdown of direct land-value contributions from nearby rewards. Positive and negative terms appear separately, so players can see how a Stadium or civic campus affects their chosen neighborhood. The report explains that these are contributions before the final 1–100 limit, not a promise of equivalent growth in final value.

[Prima official guide, reward directory p.410](https://www.scribd.com/doc/303581554/SimCity-3000-Unlimited-Prima-Guide) specifies residential/commercial/industrial strengths of 20/25/0, crime reduction 30 over radius 30, 135 jobs, and no direct aura or pollution. This milestone adds the missing value effects. A 30-tile Chebyshev value radius and linear falloff are reconstruction tuning, reusing the documented crime radius because the directory does not specify a land-value radius. Existing crime calculations retain their separate Euclidean falloff. Changes to value can indirectly affect crime and aura through the existing city formulas.

Effects require the operating full footprint and stop on fire, disconnection or demolition. Irradiated target tiles cannot gain value. Contributions are derived on recompute/load, so save schema remains 139 and older placed courthouses gain the corrected effects automatically.

The inspection groups City Hall, Mayor's House, Historic Statue and Lighthouse contributions together because the simulation currently aggregates those values. Other reward types have distinct rows. Individual-building attribution within that group is not implemented.

Focused validation covers sector strengths, no direct aura/pollution, crime interaction, stable recomputation and save reload, shutdown/rebuild, radiation, and signed/empty inspection states. Interactive browser review remains pending the locked Mac. Live publication remains pending explicit Sites repository-export authorization after automatic approval review rejection.

Completed validation: all 290 default suites passed. It operated with all 135 jobs filled and supplied direct value contributions to 132 residential tiles. Treasury remained §16,389,293. The earlier unpowered placement correctly supplied no jobs or value.
