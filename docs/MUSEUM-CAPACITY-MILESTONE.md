# Museums unlock residential growth

Operating museums now add capacity for 9,000 residents each. The residential growth report lists their contribution. Museum and library inspection panels show their active citywide capacity benefit. Power, road access, funding and strike status use the existing service operating rules; fire, rubble and radiation suppress relief. Losing capacity stops additional growth at the cap without evicting existing residents.
