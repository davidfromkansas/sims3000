# Department policy planning

## Playable milestone

Moe, Karen and Gus now offer direct ordinance comparisons from their advisor briefings. Compare carpool incentives and parking fines with transportation; clean air, clean water and trash presort with environment; or power and water conservation with utilities. Karen’s air-pollution policy shortcut opens her comparison directly.

Each department shows its relevant measures plus other measures that change. The full ordinance comparison still shows every measure. Supply comparisons include actual delivered power/water and unserved demand; recycling shows operating monthly capacity and the recyclable fraction of new garbage. These distinguish a policy benefit from repairs or facilities still needed.

Comparison preserves the city until Apply compared policies. Application uses the existing reviewed-state check, preserves unrelated policies and service funding, saves the choice, and returns to the same advisor.
