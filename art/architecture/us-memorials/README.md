# American memorial landmark batch

Original geometry is in `dist/us-memorial-models.js`. Four transparent 512×848 development renders per model are stored in the corresponding subdirectory. City and gallery views render the geometry into shared cached canvases. The models are simplified reconstructions; they do not reproduce original game assets or claim recovered original lot dimensions.

- **Washington Monument:** tapered stone obelisk, pyramidion, entrance, observation openings and a change in stone color. Reference: [NPS history](https://www.nps.gov/wamo/learn/historyculture/index.htm). Authored 3×3 footprint.
- **Gateway Arch:** a tapered triangular section following an authored inverted-catenary approximation, with stainless-steel colors. Reference: [NPS fact sheet](https://home.nps.gov/jeff/planyourvisit/gateway-arch-fact-sheet.htm). Authored 4×4 footprint. The curve is not the original structural equation.
- **Jefferson Memorial:** open circular colonnade, shallow dome, north portico, terraces and simplified standing figure. Includes 26 exterior columns, 12 portico columns and four columns in each of four openings. Reference: [NPS memorial features](https://home.nps.gov/thje/learn/historyculture/memorialfeatures.htm). Authored 4×4 footprint. Dome surface lighting was corrected after offline review exposed a radial shading seam.
- **Lincoln Memorial:** rectangular colonnade, terraced steps, attic frieze and a simplified seated figure at the entrance. Includes 36 perimeter columns and two entrance columns. Reference: [NPS exterior features](https://home.nps.gov/linc/learn/historyculture/exterior-features.htm) and [building statistics](https://www.nps.gov/linc/learn/historyculture/lincoln-memorial-building-statistics.htm). Authored 4×4 footprint.

Offline review: all four initial North views and Jefferson/Lincoln South views were inspected. The corrected Jefferson South view was inspected after the roof-lighting change. Remaining corrected views and actual in-game scale need browser review; the Mac is locked. Focused tests cover finite/unclipped depth renders in all directions, column counts, placement, uniqueness, rebuilding, scenario counting and save continuation. These are partial verification, not full visual acceptance.
