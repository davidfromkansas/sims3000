# Paint palette review

`swatches-review.png` shows the seven current material swatches, generated from `drawMaterialSwatch`'s Canvas polygon output and rasterized with Pillow. The preview colors and procedural marks come from the runtime material renderer. This is an original rendered-art review, not a browser screenshot.

Try the new material sets in Building Architect. Choose a texture, paint a visible wall or ground square, sample a material outside the filtered set, and verify that its selected swatch becomes visible. Change the model's facade and window colors to inspect the corresponding swatches. The palette's set organization is authored; the original game's full texture and paint catalog is still incomplete.
