# Chicago cultural landmarks

Original procedural miniatures of Adler Planetarium, Shedd Aquarium and the Art Institute of Chicago. Twelve four-view images are rendered by the same depth rasterizer used by the city and gallery. Transparent boundary and finite-depth checks passed; the contact sheet was visually inspected.

Architectural references:

- [Wight & Company, Adler Planetarium](https://www.wightco.com/work/adler-planetarium/): the architect's project description identifies the original twelve-sided granite structure and copper dome. This model represents that historic core, omitting the later curved glass addition.
- [Shedd Aquarium history](https://www.sheddaquarium.org/about-shedd/vision/history) and [dome restoration](https://www.sheddaquarium.org/stories/thermal-pane-dome-a-crowning-achievement): the original octagonal building, neoclassical exterior and octagonal glazed dome with trident inform this miniature. The Oceanarium and later additions are omitted.
- [Art Institute building history](https://archive.artic.edu/ryerson/armory/1) and [museum tour](https://www.artic.edu/pdf/static/download-my-museum-tour.pdf-layout-7488.pdf): the historic Michigan Avenue building and paired bronze lions inform this miniature. The Modern Wing and full campus are omitted.

These are original simplified interpretations, not extracted game assets or exact architectural surveys. Ornament, window placement, proportions and lots are authored. The three in-game footprints are 3×3, 4×4 and 4×4 respectively; original game footprints are still unverified. Architectural references establish selected recognizable features, not fidelity of every modeled detail. The project inventory separately records evidence that these buildings belong in the referenced landmark catalog.

Player review: find all three by searching Chicago in the landmark gallery; inspect each rotation, place each once, confirm duplicate placement is unavailable, save and restore the city, then demolish from a far-corner tile and rebuild. Check silhouettes at ordinary city zoom and whether entrances, roofs and lions remain recognizable. Browser and player acceptance are pending.
